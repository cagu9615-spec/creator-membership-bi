# Business Brief: CreatorHub BI

# Executive Summary
CreatorHub is a multi-channel digital business operating across subscription content, e-commerce, digital products, and high-ticket consulting. This project builds a centralized Business Intelligence (BI) data warehouse to merge fragmented customer data into unified customer profiles.

# Core Target Niches & Business Questions
1. Creator Commerce:
   - What is the total LTV per customer across all sales channels?
   - Which Substack subscribers are most likely to purchase physical merch or ebooks?
   - What is the revenue contribution of each platform?

2. Founder Communities:
   - What is the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and churn rate?
   - How many high-ticket strategy calls (Calendly) convert from active subscribers?
   - What is the retention rate across subscription cohorts?
